About

International Artist & Muralist

Our Story

Robert Maja's journey as a visual artist and muralist began in the vibrant city of Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, where he was born in September 1971. From an early age, his artistic prowess was evident, garnering recognition and praise from teachers and elders during his elementary school years.

Encouraged by this support, Robert's passion for art flourished, leading him to immerse himself further in the world of creativity. An essential chapter in Robert's artistic development unfolded as he found himself surrounded by older university art students who became his mentors.
